Help & Support

Frequently asked questions

How do I analyse an embryo image?

Open Analysis, drop an image (or click the box). The model returns stage, cellularity, ICM, TE and fragmentation within a few seconds.

How do I download the clinical report?

Check the Patient ID, Embryo ID, reviewer and comments, optionally mark the embryo as selected, then press Generate PDF Report.

What does the confidence badge mean?

It is the model's average certainty over the heads relevant to the detected stage: High ≥ 80%, Medium 60-80%, Low < 60%. Low-confidence results need closer manual review.

Is the AI output a clinical recommendation?

No. Results support laboratory documentation and expert review only. The final selection always rests with the embryologist.

Where is my data stored?

In the local SQLite database and uploads folder of the backend you are running. Nothing is sent elsewhere.
